Official personnel verification

Personnel QR Verification

Scan the QR code printed on a Brigada Onse identification card. Each card opens a permanent verification address in the form:

https://qr.brigadaonse.org/verify/<verification-token>

The verification page shows the record's current status — valid, expired, suspended or revoked — directly from the registry. Residential addresses are never shown publicly.
